SongSort

Sorts DJ show footage by song. SongSort listens to every video in your folders, works out which song is playing, and files the clip into a folder for that song.

Every clip, sorted by songPhone videos and full sets from every show, filed into a folder per song.
Hears through the mixRecognises songs even when they're pitched, sped up or under a loud crowd.
Works from DropboxReads footage straight from Dropbox and picks up new uploads as they arrive.
Built for teamsArtists, managers and content teams each see only the artists they work on.

Runs on your own computer

SongSort runs on a Mac belonging to the artist or their team. Footage, song libraries and accounts stay on that computer and in your own Dropbox. Questions: songsortapp@gmail.com.
