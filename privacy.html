Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026 · SongSort is in private beta

SongSort is a Mac app. It runs on the computer of an artist or a member of their team (the "SongSort computer"), and footage and music stay where they are. Your account, and the list of each artist's library, are kept online so they follow you to any computer you sign in on. This policy explains what information SongSort uses, where it goes, and who can see it. "We" means the makers of SongSort.

Information SongSort uses

  • Your account. Your name, email address and, if you set one, a password, plus a few settings that follow you (such as Light or Dark appearance and the owner's sorting settings). Passwords are stored only as a secure hash. When you create an account with an email and password, we send you an email to confirm the address.
  • Artists and teams. For each artist: its name, which computer sorts its footage and when it was last online, and the people on its team (their names, emails and roles).
  • Library lists. The artist's computer keeps an up-to-date list of the artist's library online: song titles, clip file names and folders, Review and "Needs a name" items, recent activity and the overview numbers. This is what the team sees on their own computers. It doesn't include the videos or music themselves.
  • Sign in with Google. If you sign in with Google, SongSort receives your name, email address and Google account ID from Google and uses them only to sign you in. It doesn't receive your Google password or access any other Google data.
  • Footage and music. The videos and song files in the folders you choose: on the SongSort computer, in a connected Dropbox account, or in Google Drive or Box folders on that computer. SongSort analyses their audio to recognise songs, copies clips into song folders, and can add song names to video filenames if you turn that on.
  • Your DJ library. If you connect rekordbox, SongSort reads the tracks and play history in that library to know which songs to listen for.
  • Logs. SongSort keeps a log of what it's doing on the SongSort computer, to help fix problems. It isn't sent anywhere unless you send it to us.

Where it's stored

Accounts, beta approvals, artists, teams and library lists are stored online with Supabase, the service we use for SongSort accounts (see Third-party services). Each artist's information can only be read by that artist's owner and the people on its team, and by our administrator account (below); these rules are enforced by the database itself. Footage, music, song fingerprints and connected Dropbox accounts stay on the SongSort computer and aren't sent to us. Each computer also keeps a copy of your account so SongSort still opens without internet, and keeps your sign-in in the Mac's Keychain.

Private beta

New accounts wait for beta access. If you email us to join the waitlist, we keep that email and what you tell us in it to manage the beta. The emails we've let in are kept online with your accounts, readable only by our administrator account. Older copies of SongSort check a list on our website instead (songsortapp.github.io, hosted by GitHub), which holds emails in scrambled form (a one-way hash); reading it is an ordinary web request, so GitHub sees your computer's IP address, as with any website.

Support and administrator access

SongSort's own administrator account (songsortapp@gmail.com) helps run the beta and support people using it. From any computer where it's signed in, it can see every account's name, email, sign-up date and when it was last used, and it can see and work on every artist as a manager: their library lists and teams, and the changes a manager can make (such as answering Review, naming songs and adding people). It isn't listed in your team. Videos play for it only where it can reach the footage, as for anyone else. On a computer where it's signed in, it can also view SongSort as another account on that computer ("Open as"). We use this access only to run the beta and support you, not for any other purpose. We have no access to your computer itself, your footage, or your passwords.

Sharing with your team

  • Your artist's team. The people an artist's owner or managers add by email see that artist in SongSort on any computer they sign in on: the library list, the team's names and emails, and what's waiting in Review. Viewers can only look; reviewers and managers can also make changes, which the artist's computer applies. Removing someone takes the artist away from them.
  • Team sharing. If an artist turns on team sharing, SongSort keeps a "SongSort Team" folder inside the cloud folder the team shares. It contains the artist's song list, clip names and folders, Review items, recent activity, and the names, emails and roles of the people on the team. Anyone that cloud folder is shared with can read it, and teammates' SongSort apps leave their changes there for the artist's SongSort to apply.
  • View-only links. If a manager creates a view-only link, SongSort makes Dropbox links to a copy of the artist's library and to each video. Anyone who has the link can see the library and watch and download those videos, without signing in. Team names and emails aren't included. Turning the link off stops the link and every video link working.

Third-party services

  • Supabase, which stores SongSort accounts, beta approvals, artists, teams and library lists, and sends account emails (Supabase Privacy Policy). SongSort's Supabase project is hosted in the Americas.
  • Google, to sign you in when you choose Sign in with Google (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Dropbox, when you connect a Dropbox account or create a view-only link (Dropbox Privacy Policy).
  • Google Drive and Box, only through their own Mac apps: SongSort reads the folders they keep on your computer and doesn't connect to those services itself.
  • Shazam, only if it's switched on for an artist: short audio snippets (about 12 seconds) from footage SongSort can't recognise are sent to Shazam to identify released records.
  • GitHub, which hosts our website and the beta list (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Claude, only if you install the SongSort extension in the Claude app and make a Claude key. Claude can then read and do what your account can in SongSort, when you ask it to. What Claude reads becomes part of your conversations with Claude and is handled under your Claude account and Anthropic's Privacy Policy. You can turn Claude keys off at any time in Settings › Account › Claude.

What SongSort doesn't do

SongSort doesn't sell or share your information, show ads, or use your information for anything other than running SongSort for you and your team. Google user data is used only for signing in, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Change your name or password, sign out, and turn Claude keys on or off in Settings › Account.
  • To have your account deleted, including everything kept online for it, email us. To have it removed from one SongSort computer only, ask that computer's owner (Settings › Team).
  • Signing out of SongSort on a computer that isn't yours removes your account's sign-in from that computer.
  • To remove SongSort and everything it stores, delete the app and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/SongSort. Your footage and song folders aren't affected.
  • To be taken off the waitlist or have your beta emails deleted, email us.

Children

SongSort isn't intended for anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is shared, we'll say so in SongSort before it takes effect.
