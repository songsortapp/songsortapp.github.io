Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

SongSort is software that runs on a computer belonging to an artist or their team (the "SongSort computer"). This policy explains what information SongSort uses and where it goes.

Information SongSort uses

  • Your account. Your name, email address and, if you set one, a password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Sign in with Google. If you sign in with Google, SongSort receives your name, email address and Google account ID from Google, and uses them only to sign you in. SongSort does not receive your Google password and does not access any other Google data.
  • Footage and music. The videos and song files in the folders the team chooses, on the SongSort computer or in a connected Dropbox account. SongSort analyses their audio to recognise songs, and copies or renames files in those folders as the team sets it up.
  • Dropbox. When a Dropbox account is connected, SongSort accesses only that account's files to read footage and file clips into song folders.

Where it's stored

Everything SongSort keeps (accounts, song fingerprints, sorting results) is stored on the SongSort computer. It isn't sent to the makers of SongSort or to anyone else.

Third-party services

  • Google, to sign you in when you choose Sign in with Google (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Dropbox, when the team connects a Dropbox account (Dropbox Privacy Policy).
  • Shazam, only if the team switches it on for an artist: short audio snippets (about 12 seconds) from unrecognised footage are sent to Shazam to identify released records.

What SongSort doesn't do

SongSort doesn't sell or share your information, show ads, or use your information for any purpose other than running SongSort for your team. Google user data is used only for signing in, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can change your name and password, or sign out, in SongSort's Settings › Account. To have your account removed, ask the owner of your SongSort, who can remove it in Settings › Team, or email us.
